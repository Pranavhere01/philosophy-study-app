    How this works

    What the numbers mean

    Year 1 (121) and Year 2 (120) count individual IGNOU units. Each NET paper counts its 31 scheduled packets — a packet may hold several syllabus clauses, so tick it only when all are covered. Chanting uses actual round counts; a 150-minute timer is not 16 rounds. Counts are coverage, not marks, mastery or exam readiness.

    Where progress is saved

    Everything saves to this browser’s local storage — no account, no server, no uploads. To keep a copy or move between devices, use Export JSON, then Import it on the other device. Your notes and ticks are private to this browser unless you export and share them yourself.
