Details

The exam gate
An aptitude test with a published minimum of 500, plus an English requirement, then a psychological test and interview. This is one of only three objective gates found in Southeast Asia that a foreigner may actually sit.
Why the department fits
UGM's Faculty of Philosophy is Indonesia's strongest, with a doctorate spanning Western, Eastern and Religious philosophy across ethics, philosophy of science and technology, philosophy of religion and culture, and social and political philosophy.
Eligibility
India is on KNB's accredited list through the Indian Embassy in Jakarta. The GPA floor is 3.00 for master's and doctoral entry, and age caps of 21, 35 and 40 apply as at 31 December.
The recognition hurdle
Indonesia requires a KEMDIKBUD equivalency decree for a foreign degree, in place of accreditation. That is a real hurdle for a distance master's and should be confirmed first.
Language
Instruction is in Bahasa Indonesia after up to a year of compulsory BIPA. No philosophy doctoral KNB programme was verified — only the master's.
