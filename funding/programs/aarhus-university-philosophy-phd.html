Details

Pay detail
The Faculty of Arts salary scale covers theology, history of ideas and philosophy. There is also an SU PhD voucher worth DKK 7,426 in 2026 for certain enrolment models.
Permit requirement
A residence and work permit must be in place before employment begins, which takes time — start it as soon as an offer is made.
Copenhagen alternative
Copenhagen's Søren Kierkegaard Research Centre is a distinctive strength, but note that Copenhagen now charges tuition even to holders of foreign living-cost stipends.
