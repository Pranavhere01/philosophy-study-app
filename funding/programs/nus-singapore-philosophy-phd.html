Details

The academic fit
Amber Carpenter, Malcolm Keating and Saranindranath Tagore work in Indian philosophy. A department that synthesises Chinese, Indian and European traditions is not a compromise for someone whose preparation is Indian plus Western philosophy — it is the target.
Money
S$3,000/month for international PhD candidates on the Research Scholarship — about US$2,330 — rising by S$500 after the qualifying examination, plus a full tuition waiver and no bond, for four years. Rates took effect on 1 January 2026 and are open to all international students. The GRE is explicitly optional.
A free Master's route too
The same Research Scholarship funds a research master's at S$2,900/month with a full tuition waiver for two years. That is one of the very few genuinely free and stipended master's routes found anywhere in this research — a rare thing in the Anglosphere.
How admission decides
Explicitly holistically — there is no entrance examination. Test scores are optional but “significantly strengthen” an application. This is the least objective part of the NUS route, and the reason it sits alongside rather than instead of the transcript-light European systems.
Also at NUS
A FASS Scholarship in Buddhist Studies, open to all nationalities, covering Buddhist Philosophy and Ethics — a second application route within the same institution.
Deadline
1 November 2026 is the tightest deadline in this entire site.
