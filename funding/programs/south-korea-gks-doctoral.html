Details

The 80% rule is an OR
It reads “80% or above on a 100-point scale or be ranked in the top 20% of one's class”, or a CGPA of at least 2.64/4.0. NIIED's FAQ confirms the class-rank alternative explicitly — that is the escape hatch.
Age limit
Under 40, which is not a constraint here.
India's quota
47 places in 2026 — 5 embassy plus 42 university. Interviews are at the Embassy in New Delhi; the Mumbai consulate officially declines GKS.
Philosophy-specific funding
The Academy of Korean Studies funds philosophy fully — tuition plus 800,000 KRW/month. Dongguk's SRD-B covers 100% of tuition in Buddhist studies.
Closed doors
Ewha is women-only. Korea University's BK21 programme excludes philosophy.
The wording to check
The guidelines state that “online and distant-learning programs are not applicable”. That clause most likely restricts the programme being funded rather than the prior degree, but confirm it in writing with NIIED before investing in an application.
