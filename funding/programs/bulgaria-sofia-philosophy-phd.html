Details

The no-exam rule
Sofia University states that foreign citizens are admitted by Rector's order after discussion of documents and a Faculty Council decision, expressly without competitive examinations. EU citizens do sit them; you would not.
The funding trap
The tuition waiver, the stipend and a €7,000 annual allowance attach to state-subsidised places, and Sofia limits those benefits to EU, EEA and Swiss nationals. For Indian sub-continent students the English-language route offers only cheap dormitory accommodation at about €40 a month plus transport and cafeteria discounts.
What to resolve
Whether a state-funded place — and therefore the stipend — is achievable on the Bulgarian-language route, and whether the ministry scholarship is open to Indian nationals. Both are unverified.
