University of Helsinki — PhD funding

Finland · PhD · Doctoral study is free; paid doctoral positions available

← All programmes · Finland

Finland's clearest favourable fact: at doctoral level no tuition fees are charged, regardless of nationality.

The master's is a different story — €9,000–20,000 a year with waivers covering tuition only, never living costs.

Details

Doctorate only
There is no national Finnish scholarship programme at all, and the EDUFI Fellowships ended in October 2025. The doctorate works because it is free; the master's does not.
Other departments
Jyväskylä, Tampere and Turku all offer philosophy doctorates.
