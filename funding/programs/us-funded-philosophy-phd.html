Details

The thesis problem
The writing sample is the decisive document — 10 to 15 pages at Oregon, 15 to 25 at many departments — and a thesis is the obvious source of one. Without a dissertation you must construct a standalone philosophical writing sample, which is entirely possible but is real work and should start early.
The funded master's exception
A funded US terminal master's is achievable: Virginia Tech ($26,181 plus full remission), Florida State (minimum GA rate $19,074), Texas Tech ($16,000 plus an 85% waiver, internationals explicitly eligible), Northern Illinois (~$16,102), Georgia State (at least $11,000 plus a full waiver), Western Michigan (~$13,000) and Ohio (~$13,000). Funding deadlines fall between 2 January and 1 March.
Avoid
Verified-unfunded programmes: Tufts (~$62,964), St Andrews MLitt (£27,750), UChicago MAPH, NYU and Columbia. An unfunded US terminal master's is the standard way this profile loses two years and a large sum.
Fulbright is closed
Fulbright-Nehru Master's is open in eight named fields only, and Doctoral Research in six STEM clusters. Philosophy is absent from both. AAUW is now STEM-only and women-only. Schlumberger is women, PhD and STEM only, and India is not on its country list.
Visa change
The F-1 “duration of status” rule was abolished by a final rule of 17 July 2026, effective 15 September 2026, imposing a fixed period capped at four years. For a five-to-six-year doctorate this creates an extension requirement worth planning for.
