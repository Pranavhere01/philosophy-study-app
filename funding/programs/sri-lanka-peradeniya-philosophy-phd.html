Details

The structure
PhD by thesis only — around 80,000 words over three to five years — admitted any time, decided by a supervisor and the Faculty Higher Degrees Committee. There is no entrance examination.
Why it is cheap
SAARC-rate doctoral fees come to roughly US$1,100 all in. That is a fraction of any Western doctorate and lower than most Indian private-university options.
No funding
No scholarship open to Indian nationals could be verified. This is a self-funded route, included because the total cost is low enough to be a genuine fallback.
Other departments
Kelaniya (Pali and Buddhist Studies), the Buddhist and Pali University of Sri Lanka, and Colombo.
