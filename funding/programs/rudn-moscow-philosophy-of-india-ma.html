Details

Why it matches
If your interests are Indian philosophy, Buddhist thought and comparative work, no other programme found in this research teaches the primary languages alongside the philosophy.
No age limit, no CGPA floor
Russian legislation does not restrict applicant age, and the governing order (No. 1378 of 3 November 2020) contains no occurrence of “age” and sets no grade threshold.
No entrance examination for quota holders
RUDN's own FAQ states that quota applicants are not required to take entrance exams at RUDN.
The drawbacks
Tuition only is free — insurance and housing are on the student. Teaching is Russian-medium. And note that SPbU's Institute of Philosophy has twelve departments, none on Indian or Eastern philosophy, while MSU's philosophy doctoral budget places are limited to Russian and Belarusian citizens, so RUDN is uniquely the Indic match.
