One of only two schemes in the whole of Latin America that is genuinely open to an Indian resident in India — no residency needed, no age limit, and a real stipend plus tuition.

But 30% of the score is the undergraduate average, and postgraduate grades are explicitly inadmissible.

Details

Why it is open
The bases state the award is open to “chilenas o extranjeras, con o sin permanencia definitiva” — Chilean or foreign women and men, with or without permanent residence. You can apply from India.
The fatal clause
The bases state that intermediate grades, partial accumulated averages, incomplete studies and postgraduate grades are not accepted. A parametrised conversion of the undergraduate average carries 30% of the score. So a strong master's cannot substitute for the bachelor's here — the master's is excluded by rule. This is the opposite of the German position and the reason the two must not be lumped together.
The tuition gap
The arancel cap is CLP 2,700,000 a year, but philosophy fees at the leading universities run CLP 5,000,000–5,700,000. The university must waive the difference or the student pays it.
The second option
One university runs its own award covering 100% of tuition plus CLP 828,000/month for four years, granted to the top two admits on a published 50/25/25 rubric.
Excluded schemes
The Chilean development agency covers Latin America and the Caribbean only, and the OAS scheme requires citizenship or residence of an OAS member state — India is a permanent observer only, so every OAS lead is dead.
