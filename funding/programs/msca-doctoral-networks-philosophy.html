Why the structure suits
There is no national gate, no quota and no age limit. You apply to a specific funded project like a job.
The catch
Philosophy doctoral networks are uncommon. Set up EURAXESS alerts and watch for them.
Same logic applies to ERC
ERC-funded projects advertise doctoral positions the same way, with the same nationality-blind character.
