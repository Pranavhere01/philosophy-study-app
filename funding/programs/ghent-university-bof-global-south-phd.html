Details

The condition
24 months of co-financing from an Indian partner institution. Without that, the scheme is not open regardless of your qualifications.
Master's cost at Ghent
Non-EEA “Tuition Fee A” is €2,297.40 for 60 credits, with the EEA rate of €1,181.40 granted to OECD-DAC lowest-category countries — whether India is on the current list is unverified.
Antwerp alternative
Antwerp's dedicated Research Master of Philosophy is only €4,400, one of the cheapest philosophy master's found in Europe, and its PhD enrolment is essentially free.
