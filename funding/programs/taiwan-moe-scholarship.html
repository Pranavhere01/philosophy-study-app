Details

Three exclusions
The foreign-qualification recognition regulation excludes degrees obtained by correspondence (§10(1)) and distance-taught degrees not meeting its conditions (§10(8)), and separately requires at least eight months of physical residence for a master's. A distance M.A. fails all three.
The grade floor
The master's requirement is 3.5 on a 4.5 scale, roughly 77.8%. A 2:2-equivalent Indian bachelor's converts to about 2.5/4.5 and fails outright.
Discretion exists
A clause lets TECO apply its own standard of excellence, so it is not absolutely closed — but it is the weakest proposition in the region.
Fees
Foreign students pay international fees, not local rates.
