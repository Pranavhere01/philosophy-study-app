Details

The model
A doctorate in Germany is a paid job with social insurance and pension accrual. There is no tuition at any public university, and no entrance examination.
The gate
A supervisor with funding who wants you. That turns on a research proposal and a conversation, not on your undergraduate transcript.
Grade floors
LMU publishes a floor of 2.50 — but assessed on the master's grade, not the bachelor's. A 75% master's converts to roughly 2.15–2.5 on the German scale depending on the pass mark, so this floor is met. That is the clearest illustration in this guide of a strong master's neutralising a weak bachelor's.
Practical move
Positions appear continuously on the German academic job portals. Set up alerts now rather than waiting for the master's to be awarded.
