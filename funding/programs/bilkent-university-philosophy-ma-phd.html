Details

The package
Every graduate student in the Department of Philosophy receives a full tuition waiver, private health insurance, a monthly stipend and discounted housing. Doctoral students additionally receive meal tickets and conference funding. The department states that non-philosophy majors and international students are encouraged to apply.
The obstacle
Stated floors are BA CGPA 2.75/4.00 for the MA and 2.75 BA plus 3.00 MA for the PhD. A weak Indian bachelor's fails the bachelor's component.
Mitigation
The department uses holistic evaluation and an examination explicitly designed not to presuppose a philosophy background. The master's percentage is the number to foreground.
ALES
The national ALES examination does not apply to foreign applicants — Turkish universities exempt them, and one regulation confirms it. Bilkent also accepts the GRE in place of ALES. Note: if you take an English test for Türkiye, IELTS does not count for the YDS-equivalence requirement — the accepted list is TOEFL iBT, Cambridge C1/C2, PTE Academic and the Oxford Test of English Advanced. Plan on TOEFL.
Türkiye Bursları — now worth re-testing
The national scholarship imposes a published graduate academic-achievement floor of 75%, and it is unresolved whether that is read off the bachelor's or the master's. A 75%+ master's would meet it exactly if the master's is the degree assessed. Given that the ALES gate is waived for foreigners and that Philosophy and Ethics is an officially eligible field, this is one of the few routes where a strong master's may reopen a scheme that a weak bachelor's closed. Ask YTB in writing which degree the floor applies to.
