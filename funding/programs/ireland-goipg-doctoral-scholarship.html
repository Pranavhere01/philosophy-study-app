Details

Why this is unusual
Most national scholarships cover home fees only, leaving internationals a large annual gap. This one closes that gap explicitly.
Two conflicting reports
One research stream found GOIPG discontinued and folded into “Empower”, an institution-level invited call. Another found it live at Research Ireland and open to internationals. The practical answer is to check the current call document and ask the institution directly.
Eligibility change
A 2025 eligibility-rule change is real, but its content could not be retrieved. Verify against the official call document.
Ireland's friendly rule
The historic GOIPG rule is the most favourable found anywhere: if the applicant does not have a first-class or upper-second bachelor's, they must possess a master's degree.
