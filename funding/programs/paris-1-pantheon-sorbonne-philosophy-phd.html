Details

The language wall
ED 280's doctoral contract competition requires a project of no more than 3,000 words, plus an anonymised duplicate, written in French. Sorbonne's ED 540 requires DALF C1.
Places
ENS-PSL's ED 540 had only 3 contracts for 2026, with a project of 18,000–20,000 characters and auditions on 1 July.
Eiffel is not available
The Eiffel Excellence Scholarship lists seven eligible domains and philosophy is not among them. Do not plan around it.

Best practices

  • Confirm in writing that a distance/open master's is accepted before paying any fee.
  • Lead every application with the master's percentage and the research project — not the bachelor's.
