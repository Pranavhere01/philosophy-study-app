Details

Why it is unusual
Most UK funding covers home fees only, leaving internationals a gap of £14,000–£37,000 a year. Gates covers the full composition fee plus maintenance, airfare, visa and the immigration health surcharge.
Eligibility
Non-UK citizens only. India is fully eligible. Requires a master's at or near Distinction, and Cambridge still states an undergraduate expectation of around 67% or GPA 3.6 — so the bachelor's record remains a live risk here, unlike in the transcript-light systems.
The wider UK position, corrected
International students have been eligible for UKRI studentships since 2021-22 — the real constraint is the fee level, not eligibility. AHRC Doctoral Landscape Awards (which replaced DTPs from 2026-27) are open to internationals but capped at 30% of awards, and cover home-rate fees only. KCL tops up to full international fees; Bristol does not.
No exam anywhere in the UK
Oxford's DPhil states that applicants are not normally interviewed and needs a proposed thesis plus a willing supervisor. There is no written entrance examination.
Other named awards
Oxford Clarendon (200+ a year, all nationalities, no separate form), Ertegun, the Faculty of Philosophy Scholarships which cover fees irrespective of fee status, Weidenfeld-Hoffmann, and the Institute for Ethics in AI.
What is closed
Commonwealth PhD Scholarships exclude India on both the least-developed-country list and the high-income list. Commonwealth Master's does include India. Chevening is master's-only and requires 2,800 hours of post-degree work experience. The Cambridge Trust international studentship excludes applicants who already hold a master's.
Timing
The Graduate route was shortened to 18 months for applications made on or after 1 January 2027, but doctoral graduates retain three years — favourable here.
