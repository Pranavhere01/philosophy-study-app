Details

The decisive clause
Anexo III.1 of the convocatoria states that master's and doctoral studies are not taken into account in the scored average. The 75-point academic-trajectory criterion carries a mandatory gate of 60, and the published minimum averages in the previous round were 8.230 for ISCED 022 Humanities, 8.260 for ISCED 02 and 7.520 for ISCED 07 Engineering. A strong master's cannot fix this — the master's is excluded from the calculation by rule, so the score comes from the bachelor's alone.
The 2026 reform
FPU moved from the ministry to the Agencia Estatal de Investigación under Real Decreto 265/2026. The research entity submits the application, including the candidates — not the candidate. Evaluation is 75 points academic trajectory plus 25 for the team and supervisor.
Money, for reference
€32,500 per year of contract financing, €3,350 end-of-contract indemnity, €10,000 for stays and doctoral registration across the whole award — about €143,350 in total, up to four years. There is no age limit; instead there is a degree-recency rule.
The realistic Spanish route
A project-funded FPI or contrato predoctoral inside a well-resourced group, where the supervisor's project pays and the transcript is not scored against a national cut-off. Andalucía is the one region charging an Indian the same master's rate as a national — about €820.80 a year — against Madrid's €5,044 and Catalonia's €4,920.
Also removed
Fundación Carolina is a Latin America programme and Indian nationals appear not to be eligible.
