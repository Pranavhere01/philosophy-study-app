Details

The legal position
The Higher Education Support (Commonwealth Scholarships) Guidelines 2025 provide for both domestic and overseas students. The barrier is allocation, not entitlement.
How rationed
One university caps international nominations at 8 per academic unit per round while placing no cap on domestic nominees. Another funds about five a year, whose successful candidates each had five publications.
The gate
Australian Honours-1 equivalence, which a 2:2-equivalent Indian bachelor's does not straightforwardly meet.
Excluded scheme
Australia Awards does not include India on its eligible-country list, and the separate India scheme excludes BITS-type institutions, demands a CGPA of 7.5, and has no philosophy field.
