Genuinely oral, with English thesis options across many supervisors.

The obstacle is not the exam but the money: only about nine bursary places in the July session, and “budget without bursary” means tuition-free with no stipend — which is not a funded doctorate.

Thesis language is set per supervisor, and English is widespread across the faculty

Details

The dossier
An activity memorandum, a list of published papers, a written proposed topic matched to a specific vacant supervisor position with justification, a comprehensive bibliography, and a research project capped at 16,000 characters.
The funding reality
September 2026 offered 3 part-time places that were state-funded without a bursary, plus 30 fee-paying places at 13,100 lei/year. The July session had 17 budget places, of which just 9 carried a bursary. Check which category a place falls into before accepting it.
Supervisors with English thesis language
Mircea Dumitru (philosophical logic, philosophy of language, analytic metaphysics); Vasile-Romulus Brâncoveanu (political and moral philosophy); Cristian Ciocan (phenomenology, French and English); Valentin Sorin Costreie (philosophy of mathematics and language); Mihnea Dobre (early modern, history and philosophy of science); Daniela Jalobeanu (history and philosophy of science); Adrian Paul Iliescu (social justice, Wittgenstein).
Gate
CNRED recognition of foreign studies is mandatory. An age limit of 35 applies only to the fee waiver, not to admission.
