Details

Why this matters most
The blocker that defeats a distance master's in several Asian systems simply is not present here. That makes it the safest large award in the region.
Assessment
Applications are assessed by a “Humanities, Social Sciences and Business Studies” panel. No CGPA floor is published and there is no age limit.
Some institutions admit without a master's
HKUST, for instance, will consider doctoral entry without a master's, which again avoids the conferral-timing problem.
Eligibility
Indian nationals are expressly eligible, irrespective of country of origin.
