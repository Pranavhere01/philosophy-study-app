Details

Why this category matters
ERC and MSCA positions are salaried, nationality-blind and advert-driven. They bypass the national scholarship systems entirely.
The timing problem
A completed master's is required, so the realistic target is 2028 entry rather than the cycle immediately after your 2027 completion.
How to find them
Monitor EURAXESS and the individual departments. These positions are advertised openly and are relatively rare in philosophy.
