Details

How the interview works
Questions on the submitted dissertation project, then committee questions on the topic testing theoretical and methodological knowledge and command of the literature. Candidates are ranked in closed session, with the extent and quality of publications also counted.
One application only
You may apply to one doctoral programme only — no parallel applications within the faculty.
Nostrification required
A nostrifikácia recognition decision from an authorised Slovak authority is required for a second-degree qualification obtained abroad. This is the gating item for a distance master's.
