Details

The named awards worth pursuing
Gates Cambridge (any nationality, full cost), Clarendon at Oxford, the Rhodes Scholarship (India is a Rhodes constituency), St Andrews' St Leonard's College scholarships and the SASP doctoral pathway.
The Oxford reality
The BPhil minimum is “a first-class or strong upper second-class undergraduate degree with honours in philosophy or a closely related discipline”, most successful applicants hold a first, and the BPhil “is not suitable as a conversion course for students changing to philosophy from another subject”. The DPhil additionally requires a distinction or near-distinction in a master's-level philosophy degree. No GRE, and interviews are not normally held.
Commonwealth
India is eligible for Commonwealth Master's Scholarships but not for Commonwealth PhD Scholarships, which are restricted to least-developed countries and vulnerable states. Chevening funds master's only and requires 2,800 hours of post-degree work experience. The Cambridge Trust international studentship excludes applicants who already hold a master's.
Grade conversion
One official India table maps 2:1 to 55% for NIRF-listed institutions and 2:2 to 50%, while other institutions require 62% for a 2:1. UK ENIC assesses a four-year Indian bachelor's as an Honours degree but attaches no class. Assume 2:2 for safety.
