The master's, however, is a financial dead end at €20,400 for the Research MA in Philosophy.

Details

The model
Four years as a salaried employee with pension accrual, on a published pay scale. Nationality is irrelevant to the appointment.
The master's problem
Non-EU students do not get the €2,694 statutory rate; the institutional fee is €15,000–22,000 a year. Do the master's elsewhere and apply here for the doctorate.
Other Dutch departments
Utrecht (with the Ethics Institute and the OZSW research school), Leiden, Groningen, Radboud, VU Amsterdam and Tilburg.
