Details

The chain of failure
Distance degree → no CSCSE certificate → no pre-admission → no CSC application. This applies at doctoral level.
The national examination
The 2027 kaoyan regulation sets four written papers totalling 500 marks, sat 19–20 December 2026. But test centres require either local household registration or six consecutive months of local social insurance, so a foreign national is practically barred.
Doctoral entry is dossier-based
Application-assessment has replaced written examinations — one university abolished its unified doctoral written examination from 2026. So China is not a brute-force route even setting the certification issue aside.
If circumstances change
Four English-taught philosophy master's exist at Fudan, Renmin, Wuhan and ECNU, and Fudan names India among its students' 41 countries of origin.
