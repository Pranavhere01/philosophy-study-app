Details

The formula
Roughly 0.1 × the diploma-supplement average, plus 0.7 × (project and interview), plus 0.2 × research experience, with a published pass mark of 5 out of 10. For philosophy, the 0.1 component is itself half diploma GPA and half the final master's thesis grade — so a strong dissertation is a direct lever, and the bachelor's is largely bypassed.
One requirement worth noting
One of the two recommendation letters must come from the potential supervisor. That means making contact before applying is not optional.
The funding
Free tuition plus a monthly research stipend. ⚠ India is not eligible for Lithuania's full-degree state scholarship — only the short-term mobility scheme, which does not fund a degree.
The gap
The interview language and whether it can be sat remotely for philosophy were not verified. That is the biggest remaining unknown and the first thing to ask.
