Details

The rule
ETH's doctoral regulations key the grade assessment to the institution awarding the M.A. A strong master's from your own university therefore neutralises a weak bachelor's.
ESKAS
The Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship pays CHF 2,450/month and requires the master's completed by 30 June 2027 for ETH — not at application. India accepts self-certified documents. Verify the cut-off with the Swiss Embassy in New Delhi.
UZH top-up
At the University of Zurich the top-up to the full SNSF rate — about CHF 50,504 in year one — is mandatory for supervisors, making UZH the best-value Swiss target.
Other Swiss departments
Zurich, Geneva, Basel, Bern, Fribourg and Lausanne. Note that Bern charges CHF 2,550 per semester because the surcharge keys off where you held residence when you obtained the bachelor's, so it bites at doctoral level.
