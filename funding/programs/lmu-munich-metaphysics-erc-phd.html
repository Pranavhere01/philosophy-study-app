Details

How to apply
One PDF: motivation letter, CV with certificates, a writing sample (the master's thesis is the obvious candidate), a research-interest statement of no more than three pages, and three referees. Subject line “ERC PhD student application”.
Requirements
A master's in philosophy or philosophy of science. A physics background is an advantage on this particular project.
Timing note
The post starts October 2027 but the deadline is December 2026, so you apply before the master's is conferred. That is normal for doctoral applications, but you must be able to evidence the degree is in progress and on track.
Why it matters
This is the shape of route that suits a weak undergraduate record best: an openly advertised salaried position decided by a supervisor, not a committee weighing where you studied.
