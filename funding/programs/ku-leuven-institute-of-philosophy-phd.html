Details

The gate, now met
A hard 75% requirement plus IELTS 7.0. With a master's above 75% the grade condition is satisfied; the language test is the remaining hurdle.
The waiver trap
KU Leuven's partial fee waiver excludes India, because India is classified lower-middle-income rather than least-developed or low-income. That affects the master's, not the doctorate.
Master's cost
If you were to study the master's there, non-EEA tuition is €9,493.90 — but doctoral study is exempt from the non-EEA fee.
Funding route
Doctoral candidates hold a paid mandate or an FWO fellowship. FWO pays €2,700/month plus €3,720 a year with no nationality bar, opens 3 December 2026 and closes 1 March 2027, and evaluates a qualitative narrative rather than grade percentiles.
