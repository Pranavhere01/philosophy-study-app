Details

The offer
A PhD in Humanities and Social Sciences taught in English, with a 100% tuition waiver and QAR 9,000 a month for international candidates over 45 months. Research-assistant duties of about ten hours a week are required, and housing is not included.
The gate
A published GPA floor of 3.0/4.0 and IELTS 6.5. Since Qatar assesses the qualifying degree, a strong master's is what matters — but this floor is a real screen.
A correction worth noting
Widely circulated reports of a fully funded Qatar University graduate scholarship are contradicted by the university's own FAQ, which states plainly that there is no scholarship for graduate programmes. The funded route in Qatar is HBKU, not Qatar University.
Not strictly philosophy
This is humanities and social sciences with philosophical grounding rather than a philosophy department proper, so check the supervisory capacity against your intended project before applying.
