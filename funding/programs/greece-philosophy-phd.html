Details

The gates, assessed
A minimum master's average of 8/10, which a 75%+ master's clears. Then Hellenic NARIC recognition of both the institution and the degree, which is a formal registry check and the real obstacle for a distance master's. Then four courses and 30 ECTS across the first two years, and two peer-reviewed outputs, or one impact-factor journal article, before defence.
The publication requirement
Two peer-reviewed outputs before you may defend is a heavier bar than almost anywhere else in Europe. With grades no longer the constraint, this becomes the binding one here.
Defence logistics
Dissertations may be written in a non-Greek language with an extensive Greek summary, and defences may be held by teleconference. Applications are accepted year-round.
Funding
There is no general doctoral salary or stipend in Greece, and IKY appears effectively citizen-based — its checklist requires a military-service certificate from male candidates. Treat this as low-cost rather than funded.
