Why this fits the brute-force preference
Brazil's processo seletivo is a written subject examination set by each programme. That is exactly the objective gate this survey was looking for, and it sits on top of zero tuition.
PEC-PG
A Brazilian programme that funds students from developing countries, India among them. Verify the current call, fields covered and stipend before relying on it.
Language
Portuguese in Brazil and Spanish in Mexico are the practical barriers, though some programmes offer English-taught tracks.
