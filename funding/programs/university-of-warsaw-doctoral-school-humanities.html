Details

How the scoring works
Stage 1 is a document and research-project assessment that eliminates anyone below 50% of available points. Stage 2 is a qualification interview. There is no written subject examination in the humanities.
Pay
Warsaw pays above the statutory floor: PLN 4,346/month gross before the mid-term evaluation and PLN 5,550.50 after, for 48 months, plus PLN 1,071.15 for certified disability.
The catch
Publication record is heavily weighted — at Jagiellonian, 40 of 100 points go to documented research activity. This is the real competitive weakness for an applicant without publications.
Timing
The 2027 window should fall around April–May, immediately before a mid-2027 master's completion.
