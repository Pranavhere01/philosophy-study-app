Details

Vienna SSH Doctoral Call
More than 40 funded philosophy and law posts, with no prior supervisor required. This is rare: most of continental Europe requires a supervisor's commitment before you can apply for anything.
ÖAW DOC
€52,080 a year with no age limit, next deadline 15 September 2027. In practice it expects a publication.
