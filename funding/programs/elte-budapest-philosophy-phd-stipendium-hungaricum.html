Details

Two gates, not one
You must be nominated by the sending partner (for India, the Hungarian Embassy channel) as well as pass the university examination. Both are required.
What is covered
Tuition exemption, the monthly stipend, dormitory place or the HUF 40,000/month housing contribution, and health insurance up to HUF 65,000/year.
No age limit found
The widely repeated “PhD under 40” claim does not appear in the current call or the operational regulations.
Caveat
ELTE's Faculty of Humanities requires the master's to carry at least a “good” classification; a weaker diploma triggers a three-year moratorium.
ELTE's strengths
Twelve doctoral programmes including Analytic Philosophy, Ethics and Political Philosophy, Phenomenology, and Logic and Philosophy of Science.
