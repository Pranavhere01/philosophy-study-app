Norway's doctorate remains free and salaried.

The master's, however, is now closed: Oslo charges NOK 204,000 a year for the Philosophy master's and states it is legally barred from offering tuition-covering scholarships.

Details

PhD is the only viable level
The 2023 tuition law for non-EEA students is fully in force and indexed annually. The PhD exemption has not been withdrawn.
Other departments
Bergen's philosophy department advertised a post at NOK 568,700; NTNU is also strong.
The master's cost
NTNU humanities charges NOK 176,300/year and states plainly that it offers no scholarships covering tuition fees.
