Why it stands out
Most Russian philosophy teaching is Russian-medium. HSE is the documented English route, and it uses portfolio review rather than an entrance examination.
Note
The Open Doors Olympiad — the one genuinely clean online examination in the Russian system — has no philosophy track among its fourteen.
