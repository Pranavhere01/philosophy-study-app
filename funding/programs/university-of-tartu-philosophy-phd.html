Why it ranks first on mechanics
There is no entrance examination to sit and no minimum grade published. What you submit is assessed on its merits as a research proposal.
Strengths
Analytic philosophy, philosophy of science, and the Tartu–Moscow semiotics school.
Hard dependency
In Estonia a negative ENIC/NARIC assessment voids an offer. Get the recognition question answered in writing before applying, not after.
