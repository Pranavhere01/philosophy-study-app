Funded — PEC-PG, a CAPES/CNPq/Ministry of Foreign Affairs scholarship for developing countries
Amount
Doctoral R$ 3,100/month for 48 months, or master's R$ 2,100/month for 24 months, plus R$ 400/month health insurance and return airfare. Tuition is free because Brazilian public universities charge none.
Tuition
Free at all public universities
Decided by
Dossier — No national examination. Two universities run a written subject paper, both sat in person in Brazil; everywhere else admission is a scored dossier, several of them fully remote.
Deadline
The 2027 cycle is institution-facing — see below
Language
Portuguese, though research projects and essays may be submitted in Portuguese, Spanish or English

Details

India is on the list
CAPES Public Notice 12/2025 names India among ten eligible Asian countries, and philosophy is eligible — two philosophy programmes ran official calls, one offering three doctoral places.
The mechanism changed
In 2026 the individual applied directly to CAPES. In 2027 it is institution-facing: universities bid for quota blocks and run their own selection. The ministry's own guidance tells candidates to wait until December 2026 for the list of quota-holding institutions, then approach them.
Two applicant-friendly clauses
No revalidation or recognition of the foreign diploma is demanded, and for doctoral selection institutions are told to prioritise candidates who took both their undergraduate and master's degrees outside Brazil.
No age limit
No age criterion appears in any of the governing notices.
How selection scores
Most programmes weight the research project heavily — one leading department weights the curriculum at 30 and the project at 70, with a pass mark of 60 for a master's and 70 for a doctorate, after a blind project pre-evaluation. Another abolished its written examination after 2023.
Recognition timing
One leading university requires diploma recognition to be initiated at least six months before the application period — so around February 2027 for an August 2027 cycle. Start it early.
Language note
There is no Celpe-Bras Portuguese test centre in India; the nearest are in China, Japan and South Korea. Several universities accept TOEFL or IELTS instead, so check per programme.
