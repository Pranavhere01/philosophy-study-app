Details

The administrative gate
You cannot apply to a university directly. Everything routes through the Ministry portal, which is unusual and needs planning.
Cost if unfunded
Theoretical faculties such as arts and philosophy charge roughly GBP 1,000 one-off registration plus GBP 1,000 a year, which is affordable but not free.
Why it is included
Egypt's HDI of 0.728 sits above India's 0.685, so it passes the standard-of-living filter applied to this site.
