Details

The scoring, in full
Master's grade 3 points; scientific achievements 22; other achievements 5; research project 20; interview 50. The pass mark is 60 of 100, of which at least 30 must come from the interview.
Why this matters
A system that weights an interview at 50% and your undergraduate record at 0% is engineered to be indifferent to where you studied.
Poznań's tradition
The Poznań–Warsaw school of logic, which is a genuine strength if your interests run to logic or formal philosophy.
