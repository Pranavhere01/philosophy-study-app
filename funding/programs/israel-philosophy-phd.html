Details

The grade floors
Hebrew University requires 85/85/85 — 85 average, 85 thesis. Tel Aviv requires an 85 average with an 85 thesis; Bar-Ilan the same. These are applied to the master's, and a thesis is mandatory.
The MFA scholarship for Indian nationals
India is explicitly named as having a special arrangement: 50% of tuition capped at US$6,000, a monthly allowance for one academic year, and basic health insurance. Applicants must be under 35 and must already hold admission. The A2 visa does not permit employment.
Departmental fit
Bar-Ilan offers an M.A. in Jewish Philosophy with an associated scholarship, which is a partial-funding option rather than a full one.
Two cautions
India's Ministry of External Affairs has issued repeated advisories for Israel urging vigilance, and an Indian Embassy advisory against non-essential travel. The Rotenstreich and President's awards are also genuinely competitive.
