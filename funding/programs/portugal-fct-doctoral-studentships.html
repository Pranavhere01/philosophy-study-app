Details

Why it is not a clean win
Criterion A is 30% of the total and half of it is the academic path — so a weak bachelor's hurts directly. Applications below 3.000 out of 5.000 are not eligible at all.
What is genuinely favourable
No age limit. No interview. No examination. Third-country citizens are expressly eligible, and you may apply before enrolling. 3,628 applications produced 1,000 approvals in the recent round — about 28%.
The tuition trap
Portuguese doctoral fees are not capped and not waived. NOVA FCSH's philosophy doctorate is €5,600 total for national students and €12,000 for internationals; Coimbra charges internationals about €7,000 a year. The FCT subsidy reimburses up to €2,750, so a cheaper institution matters.
Recognition
Decreto-Lei 66/2018 governs recognition, with three routes — automático, de nível and específico — all legally equivalent. FCT additionally requires conversion of your final grade to the Portuguese scale. Whether a distance master's is treated the same is not documented.
Departmental route
University-level doctorates also hold no entrance exam: Lisbon's Faculty of Letters evaluates curriculum and documents, with interviews only where the director considers them necessary.
