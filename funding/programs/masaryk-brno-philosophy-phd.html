Details

Round 1
Document assessment: CV, publication list, a roughly five-page doctoral thesis project plus one to two pages of literature, a motivational letter, and evidence of above-average academic performance. Success limit 60 of 90.
Round 2
Oral discussion over the doctoral thesis project. Success limit 70 of 100.
One genuine advantage
Proof of the master's degree is not required for the entrance examinations — only at enrolment. That suits a mid-2027 completion exactly.
The risk
Language of instruction is Czech, the mode table states distance: No, and the scoring rewards exactly what a distance master's plus a weak bachelor's cannot supply. Charles University is the better Czech option.
