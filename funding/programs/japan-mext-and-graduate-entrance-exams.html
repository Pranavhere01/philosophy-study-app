Details

The India-specific floor
The embassy route applies a 65% bachelor's floor for Humanities and Social Science (70% general). A 2:2-equivalent Indian degree fails it.
The examination
MEXT's first screening requires both a Japanese and an English paper — the English exemption exists only for countries where English is an official language. There is no philosophy subject paper.
Three further closures
There is no English-taught philosophy programme at any of the eight leading Japanese universities; the University Recommendation General Selection route has been discontinued from academic year 2027; and the 2026 India humanities shortlist was 2 candidates out of 185.
Practical conclusion
Japanese language preparation is not worth the time for this route.
