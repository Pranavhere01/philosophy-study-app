Details

Why this is structurally different
Everywhere else the distance mode of the master's is the risk. Here the awarding institution shares the mode, which removes the mismatch that causes rejections elsewhere.
The funding problem
South Africa's national research foundation restricts Full Cost of Study to citizens and permanent residents, and sends Partial Cost of Study to only 5% of international students. Treat this as self-funded at a low fee.
Grade floors
The NRF minimum is 65% for the previous degree, and Wits requires a 70% aggregate in the master's plus a supervisor identified before you apply — both of which a 75%+ master's clears. UCT states admission is “at the discretion of the Head of Department”, decided primarily on academic performance so far. So the grade is no longer the obstacle here; the 5% international funding quota is.
Timing
UCT's deadline for non-South African applicants — 30 September — has already passed for the 2027 intake, so the realistic target is 2028. UNISA runs to its own calendar.
