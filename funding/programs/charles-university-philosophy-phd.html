Details

The scoring, in full
Dissertation proposal discussion 0–30; prior research activity 0–15; knowledge of secondary literature 0–15. There is no component that weights your grade average.
Why it beats Masaryk for a weak record
Masaryk's first round explicitly rewards “evidence of above-average academic performance” and publication. Charles simply does not ask the question.
2025 reform
The State Doctoral Examination was abolished for everyone enrolling from 1 March 2025, replaced by a dissertation pre-defence. Older guides describing “the Czech doctoral exam” no longer apply.
Critical condition
The doctoral study income is payable only in the full-time form. Combined-form students receive nothing, so full-time enrolment is essential.
Nostrification is mandatory
Charles states plainly: “Without having finished the nostrification/recognition process, you cannot be enrolled.” Faculty recognition takes up to 30 days and costs CZK 930; full nostrification up to two months and CZK 3,000, with lifelong validity. Start it early.
Language
The Czech-language track is free; the English track charges CZK 25,000/year, though the stipend covers that within about three weeks.
