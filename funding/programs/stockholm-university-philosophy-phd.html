Details

The ladder
A new collective agreement took effect on 1 October 2026 and is time-based on net study time. The 2025 ladder was SEK 31,600 / 33,300 / 34,700, so the trajectory is rising.
Other Swedish departments
Uppsala, Lund and Gothenburg are all strong, and KTH's ladder has been independently verified.
Excluded scheme
The Swedish Institute's SISGP is not open to India — India is not among its 34 eligible countries. Ignore it; the salaried positions remain open.
