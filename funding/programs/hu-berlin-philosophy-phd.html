Grade floors
Published floors key off the master's grade. FU Berlin's stated floor is “gut”, roughly 2.5 on the German scale — and a 75% master's converts to about 2.15–2.5, so it is met. Berlin has three strong departments, HU, FU and TU, which cooperate through the Berlin Philosophical Colloquium.
