Details

Departmental fit
USM lists Philosophy explicitly as a research area — Asian philosophy, comparative philosophy, epistemology, ethics, Islamic philosophy, metaphysics, philosophy of religion and philosophy of science. Universiti Malaya's Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences also supervises philosophy, and IIUM offers a PhD in Islamic Thought and Civilisation.
MIS eligibility
India is eligible. Age limits are 40 for a master's and 45 for a doctorate, and doctoral applicants need a master's at CGPA 3.00. Arts and Humanities is a priority area.
The low-friction door
A coursework master's at CGPA 2.50–2.99 can still enter a doctorate if you satisfy one of several alternatives — a year of work experience, a publication, an interview, or a special assessment. That is a genuine second chance route for a distance master's.
The recognition requirement
Malaysia requires the qualification be “recognised as equivalent by the Government of Malaysia”, which is a real hurdle for a distance master's. Confirm it in writing before applying.
Correction
Universiti Malaya has no philosophy department — an earlier draft listed it wrongly. Its prospectus contains no philosophy programme; the occurrences of “philosophy” there are course components elsewhere, such as Chinese Philosophy within the Master of Chinese Studies. USM is the verified Malaysian philosophy programme.
