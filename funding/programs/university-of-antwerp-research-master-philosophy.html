Why it matters here
Several of the strongest doctoral routes require a research master's with a real thesis — Quebec's PBEEE says so explicitly, and Australia and New Zealand effectively require it. A €4,400 research master's is a cheap way to acquire one.
The doctorate
Enrolment is €591 on first registration and free thereafter, and doctoral candidates hold paid mandates or FWO fellowships. FWO pays €2,700/month plus €3,720 a year with no nationality bar.
Compare with Ghent
Ghent's non-EEA Tuition Fee A is €2,297.40 for 60 credits, with the EEA rate of €1,181.40 granted to OECD-DAC lowest-category countries — whether India qualifies is unverified.
