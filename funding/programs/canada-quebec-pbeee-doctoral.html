Details

Why the thesis requirement matters
This is one of the clearest financial arguments for undertaking a dissertation during the M.A. rather than taking six taught electives. An essay or research report does not satisfy it.
The federal picture
No federal Canadian money is reachable from outside the country. Vanier CGS has been abolished, and the tri-agency system replaced by CRTAS: CGRS-M is for citizens and permanent residents only, and CGRS-D requires you to be already registered in a doctoral programme by the application deadline. Ontario Trillium is defunct.
York University as a stepping stone
York's Philosophy MA is funded for both domestic and international students, with the differential fee absorbed by the fellowship, and a deadline of 15 January 2027. Calgary guarantees internationals $25,455 — higher than the domestic package.
The candid departments
Queen's, Waterloo, Dalhousie, McMaster and Guelph all state in their own words that they cannot fund or readily admit international students. McGill has no general Philosophy MA at all.
Language
No English-test waiver is available for a distance degree — India is on no exemption list, and Waterloo and Carleton expressly reject medium-of-instruction letters. Target IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 100 with every section at 23 or above.

Best practices

  • Confirm in writing that a distance/open master's is accepted before paying any fee.
  • Lead every application with the master's percentage and the research project — not the bachelor's.
