Details

Heinrich Böll Foundation
The best tuition-reimbursing option in Germany — €1,500/month for doctoral candidates plus tuition reimbursed up to €10,000 a year. It is politically affiliated to the Greens and applicants should be comfortable with that.
Note
Böll is a scholarship on top of admission, not an alternative to it — you still need a supervisor.
